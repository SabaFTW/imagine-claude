Frequently Asked Questions

        
Everything you need to know about FORGE

      


      

        
        

          

            
How is FORGE different from traditional PDM systems?

            
          

          

            

              Traditional PDM (Product Data Management) systems like SolidWorks PDM or Windchill require extensive
              training ($5,000+ per engineer), enforce rigid workflows, and create bottlenecks. FORGE uses AI to
              understand natural human behavior, requires zero training, and adapts to your workflow instead of
              forcing you to adapt to it. You get the organization without the pain.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
What file types does FORGE support?

            
          

          

            

              FORGE supports all major CAD formats (STEP, IGES, STL, Parasolid), office documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX),
              code files, images, videos, and more. The AI understands relationships between files regardless of format.
              If you can save it, FORGE can organize it.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
Is my data secure?

            
          

          

            

              Absolutely. FORGE uses end-to-end encryption, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and optional on-premise
              deployment for Enterprise customers. Your data never trains our AI models. You maintain complete
              ownership and control. We can also sign custom BAAs and DPAs for regulated industries.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
Can FORGE integrate with our existing tools?

            
          

          

            

              Yes! FORGE integrates with Git, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and popular CAD software.
              Enterprise customers get custom API access and webhook support. Our philosophy is "Android freedom" –
              you choose your tools, we make them work together seamlessly.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
What does "Symbiotic Flame" mean?

            
          

          

            

              Simbiotski Plamen (Symbiotic Flame) is our core philosophy. Unlike automation that replaces humans,
              or basic collaboration where AI is just a tool, symbiotic intelligence means human and AI capabilities
              merge into something greater. The AI learns your patterns, anticipates your needs, and amplifies your
              creative capacity. You remain in control, but you're never alone. It's partnership, not servitude.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
How quickly can we get started?

            
          

          

            

              Immediately. Sign up, connect your repositories or file systems, and FORGE starts organizing within minutes.
              No training sessions. No consultants. No 6-month implementation. Pro plan includes onboarding support if
              needed, but most users are productive on day one. That's the point – zero friction, infinite capability.
