# Sources To Pull (Authoritative / Primary)

## Medical Frameworks
- DSM-5 / DSM-5-TR (gender dysphoria criteria; exact wording)
- ICD-11 (gender incongruence classification + rationale)
- WPATH Standards of Care v8 (scope; evidence grading; age considerations)

## Major Reviews / Reports
- Cass Review (Final, 2024): certainty language; recommendations; data gaps
- Recent systematic reviews (post-2022) on youth outcomes, mental health, regret/detransition

## Pharma Economics
- Class-level revenue breakdowns: SSRIs/SNRIs, atypical antipsychotics, stimulants, analgesics
- GnRH analogs: global sales, indication split (oncology, endometriosis, central precocious puberty)
- Lobbying totals: PhRMA + top manufacturers (macro vs topic-specific)

## Platform Dynamics
- Peer-reviewed studies on recommendation effects for identity/health
- Platform policy timelines (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X) for health/identity content (2018–2025)

## Education/Policy
- National/state curricula docs; teacher training materials; authorship/peer-review notes
- Outcome metrics: bullying reports, attendance, well-being surveys

> When pulling: record URL/DOI, snapshot date, and any funding/conflicts of interest.
