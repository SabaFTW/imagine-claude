# Pharma, Policy, Platforms & Outcomes — Research Dossier (v0.1)
**Scope:** Investigate verifiable patterns without pathologizing identities or assuming intent.
**Method:** Grounded • Curious • Honest • Rigorous ("Warm Rigor")

## Why this exists
Public debate is noisy. We separate:
- **Documented** vs **Contested** vs **Unknown**
- **Economic incentives** vs **Clinical ethics** vs **Platform dynamics** vs **Education policy**

## Questions
1. Where do pharma profits actually come from (by class)?
2. What changed in diagnostic/treatment patterns and when?
3. Who funds what (industry, foundations, gov), with what documented incentives?
4. What do outcomes data say (benefits, harms, uncertainties), especially for youth?
5. How do social platforms and algorithms shape visibility and discourse?
6. What's in curricula, who authors it, and with what review/oversight?

## Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)
- Do **not** conflate LGBTQ+ existence with pathology.
- Critique **systems/incentives**, not people.
- Name uncertainties; don't cherry-pick.
- Center the patient's welfare, informed consent, and supportive care.

## Evidence Rating
- **A**: Multiple high-quality RCTs/meta-analyses with consistent effects
- **B**: Strong observational evidence, replicated, plausible mechanisms
- **C**: Mixed/limited; short follow-ups; attrition/measurement issues
- **D**: Anecdotes/single studies/opaque methods

## Initial Landscape (pre-verification snapshots)
- **Pharma economics:** Historically dominated by antidepressants, antipsychotics, stimulants, analgesics. GnRH analogs + cross-sex hormones are **small** relative markets; incentives exist, but scale ≪ blockbusters.
- **Diagnostic frameworks:** DSM-5 (2013) → "Gender Dysphoria" (distress-focused). ICD-11 moved "Gender incongruence" out of mental disorders to sexual health (2022). Youth mental-health diagnoses rose in 2010s; **multifactorial** drivers.
- **Outcomes:** Studies often show improvements in mental health for recipients of gender-affirming care; **long-term high-certainty evidence is limited for youth**. Detransition exists; support must be non-stigmatizing; definitions vary.
- **Platforms:** Engagement-ranking amplifies identity/health discourse; exposure ≠ causation. Causal links to clinical outcomes remain **uncertain**.
- **Education/policy:** Varies by jurisdiction; typically framed as anti-bullying/health literacy/civics. Map authorship, review, and outcome measures.
